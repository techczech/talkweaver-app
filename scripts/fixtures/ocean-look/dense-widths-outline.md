# Dense widths

## Running text

### A long title that runs across most of the width of the slide and wraps

The committee met on Tuesday to review the second draft of the shared reading list, agreed on three changes to the order of the sessions and asked for a shorter summary before the next meeting.

### Points with full sentences

- Each group reads the same short chapter before the session and brings one question they could not answer alone.
- The facilitator collects the questions at the start, sorts them into themes and leaves the hardest one for the end.
- Notes from every session go into the shared folder by Friday, with the open questions marked for the next week.
- Příliš žluťoučký kůň úpěl ďábelské ódy, zatímco studenti procházeli poznámky a hledali společné otázky.

### Numbers in running text

In 2024 the course had 148 participants in 12 groups; in 2025 it grew to 236 participants in 19 groups, and the average session lasted 87 minutes.

### Two columns of short points {columns}

**Before the session**

- Read the chapter
- Write one question
- Bring a printed copy

**After the session**

- Post the notes
- Mark open questions
- Book the next room
