# TalkWeaver demo vault

Twelve small talks that show what TalkWeaver can do. Open them to see a feature working, then copy a slide or a whole talk into your own vault.

## Add it to TalkWeaver

1. Download and unzip this folder somewhere you keep documents, for example `Documents`.
2. In TalkWeaver, open the Talks panel and choose **Add vault…**.
3. Choose **Choose a folder…** and pick the unzipped folder (`TalkWeaver demo vault` from the release download, or `demo-vault` in a copy of the source).

The talks appear in the Talks panel. TalkWeaver writes its own small settings file inside the folder; the talks themselves are plain Markdown outlines with their pictures, sounds and videos beside them. The pictures are generated placeholders.

## The talks

- `ai-chat-and-terminal-slides/` shows the chat and terminal simulation slides, including the lanes view.
- `embedded-page/` shows `[Embed: …]` with a local web page: a click counter and a page with a text box, a drop-down and a long list.
- `emphasis-and-audio/` shows the four inline marks (bold, underline, highlight, strikethrough) on light and dark slides, and audio files on a slide.
- `emphasis-on-next/` shows `{emphasis-steps}`, where each mark appears on its own press of Next.
- `layout-sampler/` shows every registered layout in one deck, with a speaker-note line for each.
- `layout-showcase/` shows the main layout families: titles, text, icons, images, grids, diagrams and charts.
- `link-checks/` shows web links, an action button that opens a page kept in the talk folder, a board and My Notes.
- `one-rendering-model/` shows one layout rule per slide; the speaker notes name the rule.
- `play-on-next/` shows `{play-on-next}`, where a video or sound starts on a press of Next.
- `pre-work-workshop/` shows pre-work with all five step kinds, kept in a work file and named by the talk, a post-work survey, a board filled from pre-work answers, results slides, a custom reaction set and a keep/change/try board.
- `step-through-images/` shows the talk-level `image_steps` setting, where Next enlarges each image in turn with its label.
- `testing-live-polls/` is a bare outline for trying live polls.

## Work files

- `Work files/pre-work-workshop-intake.md` holds the five pre-work steps of the pre-work workshop. The talk names it with `prework:`.
- `Work files/pre-work-workshop-after.md` is a two-question survey about what was learnt, made from the "Survey what was learnt" starting point. The talk names it with `postwork:`.

## Notes

- Presenting files (`*-present.html`) and slide version history are made by TalkWeaver on your machine and are not included.
- Some talks link to `https://example.com`; those links are placeholders.
