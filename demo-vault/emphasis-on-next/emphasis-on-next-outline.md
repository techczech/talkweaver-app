# Emphasis on Next

## Emphasis steps
{id=emphasis}

### Four marks, four presses of Next
{emphasis-steps}

The plan has one **key phrase** and one ++underlined phrase++ that appear in order.

Then a ==highlighted claim== comes in, and last a ~~struck-out idea~~ closes the slide.

### A reveal list with emphasis
{plainlist}{reveal}{emphasis-steps}

- The first item opens with a **bold phrase** and then carries on for a little while
- The middle item has no emphasis at all and simply stays as ordinary text
- The third item closes with a second **bold phrase** and some trailing words

### Same marks, no option: everything shows at once

The plan has one **key phrase** and one ++underlined phrase++ here.

Then a ==highlighted claim== comes in, and a ~~struck-out idea~~ closes the slide.

### A **bold title** shows at once
{emphasis-steps}

The body has one ==highlighted claim== that appears on Next.
