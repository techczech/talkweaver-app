- From this version on, the Mac app updates with one click: press Update in the status bar when a new version is out.
- Settings › Set-up folder: choose a folder that syncs between your Macs, and your preferences and shortcuts follow you.
- On two Macs, TalkWeaver says which Mac holds a recording and names sync conflicts instead of tripping over them.
- TalkWeaver never saves over a file it cannot read, such as a recording or Notes file left in conflict by a sync.
- When two Macs change a recording's edits, both versions are kept. Kept edits in the recording editor opens or restores either one.
- Settings › About and updates › Get early versions (alpha) offers early versions as well as releases. It is off by default.
- Editing: Enter at the start of a slide title adds a line above it, there is always a line after an object, and an object deletes in one step.
- Icon rows with a lot of text keep their panels with slightly smaller type. The presenter preview keeps step with the audience, and exported videos lose the stray "Slide" chapter.
