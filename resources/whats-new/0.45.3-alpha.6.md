# What's new in 0.45.3-alpha.6

- The start screen now fills the window. Under **Open a talk**, search all your talks, see up to eight recent talks with slide counts and dates, or choose **Show all** or **Example talks**.
- Beside it, **Start a new talk** lets you name the talk, start with a slide or an outline, write in Markdown, import PowerPoint or change where new talks are saved.
- The Research button has moved off the start screen. Once a talk is open, use Research in the sidebar, press ⌘⇧R or find it in the command palette.
- The **Talks** heading hides when the sidebar gets too narrow to show it in full.
- Behind the scenes, we reorganised how TalkWeaver handles preferences and slide styles. Your talks stay the same.
