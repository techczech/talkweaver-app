# Recording in the presenter view

- In the presenter view, use Record in the top status bar. Choose Slides and voice or Slides, voice and camera. Voice, slides and camera are saved as separate layers in the Run.
- Open the Microphone menu and turn Preview on to check your devices without saving a recording. Devices open when you open that menu, enable Preview, check devices or press Record.
- Pause and resume as needed. Mark a mistake (Shift+M) adds a marker. Start again asks before discarding the current take; earlier saved Runs are kept. Refresh reloads the slides and resumes the recording afterwards; the gap is noted in the Run.
- After Stop, the Mac writes out your words in the background. Built-in words need macOS 26. In Settings › Recording, choose This Mac (built in) under Words written out by, and choose the language you will speak.
- Precise word timing needs a model. In Settings › Recording › Precise word timing, use Download for English or Install from file… for Czech. The Czech file on this Mac is ~/Library/Application Support/talk-weaver/alignment-models-to-install/czech-model-int8-f6d12c64.onnx.
- After installing a model, new words are aligned automatically. For an earlier Run, open it in Studio and choose Align words again. Earlier timings are kept where alignment is uncertain.
- Edit video opens the saved Run in Studio, where it plays back: the slides, your camera in the bottom-right corner and your voice. Click a slide number under the picture to jump to it.
- Trim start here and Trim end here (or the keys I and O) set where the video begins and ends at the playhead; you can also drag the handles on the bar under the picture. Undo and Redo (⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z) and Use the whole recording put it back, also after you reopen the Run. A long silence at the start or end is offered as a trim; nothing is cut until you click. The recording itself is never changed.
- Export video… saves one MP4 where you choose, with chapters from your slides and, if you like, a captions file, a chapter list for YouTube and the sound on its own. The Camera switch decides whether your camera is in it. A Run without the slides picture offers Export sound….
