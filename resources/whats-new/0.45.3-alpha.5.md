# What's new in 0.45.3-alpha.5

- In Slide view, press Page Down or ⌘↓ for the next slide, and Page Up or ⌘↑ for the previous slide. Find both commands in the View menu, command palette and shortcut sheet.
- On a slide with points and one pasted picture, the points now sit beside the picture and stay on the slide.
- Closing slides in Classic and Green repeat the opening slide’s icon.
- Table text in Outline is readable in dark mode, and small text on picture, video and web page cards is clearer.
- Pathways has an Add selected button and an icon on its tab. Long pathway titles wrap to two lines.
- The Reveal badge sits clear of sidebar slide titles and shows the correct number of steps. The Insert here label is easier to read.
- The talk title stays readable in a narrow title bar when a pathway is selected. Long slide titles in the right panel stop at two lines.
