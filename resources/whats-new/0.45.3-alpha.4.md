# What's new in 0.45.3-alpha.4

- Edit the current slide in the Slide text panel beneath the slide. See its object cards, keep the Inspector on that slide and place the caret where you click in speaker notes.
- Present opens Presenter View. Use single-screen presentation when you have one display.
- Edit speaker notes in place and preview slide objects in Outline and Markdown. Reveal previews show the full slide, with a Steps row for checking the sequence.
- Closing slides match the title theme and repeat its icon in Ocean, Ocean bold amber, Forest and Slate. Changing title options keeps the design, and the Picture controls fit better in narrow windows.
- Open Pathways from its own tab or switch to it from the title bar. The sidebar keeps the current slide at the top, wraps titles and marks insertions with a dotted line. The preview hides when you close the right panel.
- Outline stays within the window with a narrower gutter. The app gives the macOS window controls room and groups its Outlining and Formatting commands separately.
- Insert several Scratchpad items together in the order they appear. Undo works across saves and keeps track of the right slides, with less memory used while you edit. Autosave resumes when you dismiss the options opened by typing `{`.
