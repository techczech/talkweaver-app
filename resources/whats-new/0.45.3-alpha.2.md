# What's new in 0.45.3-alpha.2

- Write and edit your whole talk in Markdown view.
- Turn a sketch into slides. Open Scratchpad from its own tab in the right panel.
- Undo keeps your place in the right panel, and Escape closes it.
- TalkWeaver restarts its file watcher if it stops and picks up outside changes within about a second, even when macOS is late to report them.
- Press Enter at the end of a slide title in Markdown to move into the slide body without adding an extra blank line.
- After inserting a table from three backticks, Undo removes the table and restores the backticks.
