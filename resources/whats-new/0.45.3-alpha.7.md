# What's new in 0.45.3-alpha.7

- Every video has one control bar with **Back to start**, **Play/Pause**, the time and a scrub bar. Use it in Slide view, in the right panel and in presenter view.
- In presenter view, press **0** to restart the video, **comma** to go back 5 seconds or **full stop** to go forward 5 seconds. Scrubbing also moves the video in the audience window. If no audience window is open, the sound plays in presenter view.
- Add a marker at the current playback point from **Video** in the right panel and give it a name. It’s saved on one line under the video, for example `[Marker: 1:23 The demo]`. In presenter view, markers appear as numbered ticks and in the **Markers** list. Press **1–9** to jump to them.
- **Show all designs** offers more layouts for list slides and checks that each suggested layout fits your text. When a design changes the slide’s structure, it tells you what changes.
- In a narrow sidebar, the buttons no longer cover **Outline**.
- On a freshly started Windows PC, publishing your first handout no longer says another publish is running.
- Layout checks wait until you stop moving through slides and check only the designs the panel shows.
- Behind the scenes, we reorganised file handling and merging and tidied the tests and release tools.
