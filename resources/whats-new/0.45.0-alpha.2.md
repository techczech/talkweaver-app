# What's new in 0.45.0-alpha.2

- **One-click updates.** When a new version is out, press Update in the status bar: TalkWeaver downloads it, checks it and restarts into it. Nothing downloads until you press it, and the restart waits while you present, record, export or publish.
- **Get early versions.** Settings › About and updates › Get early versions (alpha) offers alphas as well as releases. It is off by default.
- **Editing.** Enter at the very start of a slide title adds a line above it; there is always a line under an object at the end of a slide; an object deletes in one action, with a Delete button.
- **Icon rows keep their panels** when they hold a lot of text, with slightly smaller type.
