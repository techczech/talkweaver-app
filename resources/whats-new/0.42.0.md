# What's new in 0.42.0

- **Slide Scratchpad.** Keep candidate slides and visuals beside your talk, compare them, and insert a whole slide or just its visual when you are ready. Insert several selected candidates as slides in one Undo step, and save your own card order.
- **Set slides aside.** Move selected slides into the Scratchpad and drag them back into the grid or outline. Undo restores slides removed from the talk.
- **Faster slide navigation.** Thumbnails appear progressively, grid moves keep pictures and your place on screen, and keyboard navigation follows the active slide immediately.
- **Search slide contents.** Find words inside slides from the outline or grid, see matching text, and move between results.
- **Object cards in the editor.** Tables, diagrams, code, embedded pages, notes and comments have cards with their own editing controls.
- **Web pages while presenting.** Interact with a page, save views, and freeze it to use Pointer or Pen. Return Live shows the live page again.
- **Settings in their own window.** Search settings and commands, customise shortcuts, and keep the editor open alongside them.
