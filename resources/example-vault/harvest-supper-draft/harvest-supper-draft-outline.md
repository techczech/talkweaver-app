## 08 Manage talks
{id=08-manage-talks}

### Tools and windows
{id=h3}

> **Try it.** In All commands (<kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>): **TalkWeaver Studio…**, **TalkWeaver History…** and **TalkWeaver Importer…**.

> **Try it.** In Studio, <kbd>⌘\</kbd> hides the recordings. In the Importer: <kbd>F</kbd> flagged slides, <kbd>⌘F</kbd> search, <kbd>⌘↵</kbd> apply, <kbd>⌥R</kbd> reset, <kbd>⌘1</kbd> views.

> **Try it.** <kbd>⌘N</kbd> opens a new window, and so does **Open selected talk or folder in a new window** (<kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>).

### The Talks panel
{id=h4}

> **Try it.** In All commands (<kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>): **New presentation…**, **New folder…** and **Refresh talk list**.

> **Try it.** Click a talk in the Talks panel. <kbd>V</kbd> switches the view, <kbd>N</kbd> shows titles or file names, <kbd>/</kbd> filters, <kbd>S</kbd> sorts, <kbd>⌘O</kbd> opens.

> **Try it.** <kbd>⌘↑</kbd> goes up a folder, <kbd>⌘←</kbd> folds every subfolder, <kbd>F2</kbd> renames, <kbd>M</kbd> moves a talk into a folder.

### Copy, then Bin
{id=h5}

> **Try it.** On Harvest supper (draft) in the Talks panel, <kbd>⌘D</kbd> makes a copy. On the copy, <kbd>⌘⌫</kbd> moves it to the Bin once you confirm.

> **Try it.** **Add vault…** (<kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>, or at the foot of the sidebar) adds a folder of your own talks.

> **Try it.** <kbd>N</kbd>, <kbd>S</kbd>, <kbd>R</kbd> and <kbd>C</kbd> work in Manage notes, which opens from a recording in Studio. This vault has no recording.

## 09 Check and repair
{id=09-check-and-repair}

> **Try it.** In All commands (<kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>), **Optimise images to WebP** makes pictures smaller, **Index image text** makes words in pictures searchable, **Check embeds** tests videos and sites.

> **Try it.** **Layout Doctor** (<kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>) lists two problems in this talk: the missing picture and the empty slide. **Normalize trigger lines** tidies the layout lines under titles.

## 10 Set up TalkWeaver
{id=10-set-up-talkweaver}

> **Try it.** <kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd> opens All commands; <kbd>⌘K</kbd> opens Actions here, for where the caret is. <kbd>⌘/</kbd> or <kbd>⌃/</kbd> shows every key.

> **Try it.** <kbd>?</kbd> shows the keys in Pathways, Manage notes and the Importer. <kbd>⌘,</kbd> opens Settings; Cloudflare is there.

> **Try it.** In <kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>: **Get started: open the Guide**, **Get started: show the Guide button**, **Get started: show the example vault again**.
