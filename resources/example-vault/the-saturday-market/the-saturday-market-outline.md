# The Saturday market

## 00 Start here
{id=00-start-here}

### Start here
{id=s1}
{statement}

This talk is the example. Each section teaches the commands for one task, on slides you can change freely.

> **Try it.** Click Guide at the foot of the left sidebar and choose a chapter. Nothing here can be broken: <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> undoes.

## 01 Write the outline
{id=01-write-the-outline}

### Fresh every week
{id=s2}

Bread and honey arrive fresh. See the market page.

> **Try it.** Select fresh and press <kbd>⌘B</kbd>. In All commands (<kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>) try **Italic**, **Inline code**, **Highlight**, **Strikethrough** and **Underline**.

> **Try it.** Select market page and press <kbd>⌘⇧L</kbd> to make it a link.

### Rain or shine
{id=s5}

> **Try it.** In All commands (<kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>) choose **Fold all sections**, then **Unfold all sections**.

> **Try it.** Choose **New slide** there too. On its heading, <kbd>⌘⇧←</kbd> moves it up a level and <kbd>⌘⇧→</kbd> back down.

### Who runs it
{id=s6}

A rota of six

> **Try it.** On A rota of six, <kbd>⌘⌥↑</kbd> makes a heading like Who runs it; <kbd>⌘⌥↓</kbd> makes one a level below.

> **Try it.** On Who runs it, <kbd>⌘⌥⇧→</kbd> moves it and every heading under it down a level; <kbd>⌘⌥⇧←</kbd> moves them back.

### A stall of your own
{id=s7}

Ten pounds a morning, tables lent free.

> **Try it.** In this title, <kbd>↵</kbd> takes the caret to the first line below. At the end of the title, <kbd>⌘⌫</kbd> clears the words but keeps the slide.

> **Try it.** Select the line above and choose **Bulleted list** or **Numbered list** in All commands (<kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>).

### What to bring
{id=s8}

- A shopping bag
- Small change

> **Try it.** At the end of Small change, press <kbd>↵</kbd> for a new item, <kbd>Tab</kbd> to indent it and <kbd>⇧Tab</kbd> to bring it back.

> **Try it.** In a quotation, <kbd>⇧↵</kbd> starts a new paragraph and <kbd>↵</kbd> twice leaves it.

### Four stalls
{id=s3}

- Bread and cakes
- Vegetables
- Plants
- Second-hand books

> **Try it: move a slide.** Put the caret on the heading above and press <kbd>⌘⇧↓</kbd>. The whole slide moves below **Opening hours**.

> **Try it.** <kbd>⌘⇧↑</kbd> moves it back, <kbd>⌘⇧⌫</kbd> deletes it, <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> undoes, <kbd>⇧⌘Z</kbd> redoes.

### Opening hours
{id=s4}

Saturdays, eight until one.

## 02 Build a slide
{id=02-build-a-slide}

### Choose a layout
{id=s9}

> **Try it.** <kbd>⌘P</kbd> shows the Inspector; there, <kbd>⌥↑</kbd> <kbd>⌥↓</kbd> change slide and <kbd>⌥←</kbd> <kbd>⌥→</kbd> step through it. <kbd>⌘L</kbd> shows this slide in each layout.

> **Try it.** In All commands (<kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>), **Board slide** adds a board slide after this one.

### Pictures and icons
{id=s10}

- Bread
- Flowers

> **Try it.** On Flowers, press <kbd>⌘I</kbd> to pick an icon. <kbd>⌘⇧I</kbd> searches pictures recovered from earlier PowerPoint files.

### Stall fees
{id=s11}

| Stall | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Table | £10 |

> **Try it.** Move the caret onto the table and press <kbd>↵</kbd> to edit it, <kbd>⌘↵</kbd> to finish. <kbd>⇧⌘M</kbd> shows how it is written.

> **Try it.** On the id line under the title, <kbd>↵</kbd> moves the caret into the slide.

### Add something
{id=s12}

> **Try it.** In All commands (<kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>) choose **Insert table**, **Insert mindmap**, **Insert Chart**, **Insert Mermaid diagram**, **Insert diagram…** or **Insert SVG**.

> **Try it.** The same list (<kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>) has **Insert Code**, **Insert QR code**, **Insert Action button**, **Insert embed / simulation**, **Insert auto-embed** or **Insert countdown**.

## 04 See the talk

### The whole talk
{id=s13}

> **Try it.** <kbd>⌘1</kbd> editor only, <kbd>⌘2</kbd> editor and slides, <kbd>⌘3</kbd> slide strip, <kbd>⌘4</kbd> grid. If a slide looks out of date, choose **Rebuild preview** in All commands (<kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>).

### Find your way
{id=s16}

> **Try it.** <kbd>⌘⇧F</kbd> works on this slide alone; Esc goes back. <kbd>⌘⌥←</kbd> and <kbd>⌘⌥→</kbd> jump to the heading before or after.

> **Try it.** <kbd>⌘⇧O</kbd> searches the slide outline, <kbd>⌘⇧[</kbd> hides or shows the sidebar, and <kbd>⌘F</kbd> finds words.
