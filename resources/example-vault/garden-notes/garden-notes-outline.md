# Garden notes

## Our plot
{id=our-plot}

### Where the plot is
{id=g1}

Behind the station, through the green gate. Twelve beds, one shed and a water butt.

### What we grow
{id=g2}

- Salad leaves
- Beans and peas
- Herbs
- Flowers for cutting

### Seedlings in May
{id=s15}

Tomatoes, beans and courgettes go out after the last frost.

### The plant stall
{id=s14}

- Seedlings in trays
- Herbs in pots
- Cut flowers in summer

### Opening hours
{id=s4}

Saturdays, eight until one.

## Through the year
{id=through-the-year}

### Spring jobs
{id=g3}

- Dig in the compost
- Sow under cover
- Mend the fence

### Autumn jobs
{id=g4}

- Save seed
- Plant garlic
- Cover the empty beds
