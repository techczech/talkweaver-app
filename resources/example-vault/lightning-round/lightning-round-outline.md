# Lightning round

## 06 Present
{id=06-present}

### Move through the talk
{id=s21}

Open the presenter with <kbd>F5</kbd>, or from this slide with <kbd>⇧F5</kbd>.

> **Try it.** There, <kbd>F5</kbd> opens the audience window, <kbd>⌘E</kbd> goes back to the slide in the editor, <kbd>⌘R</kbd> brings in edits. <kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd> opens All commands, which lists every presenter command, key or no key.

> **Try it.** Press <kbd>→</kbd> for the next slide, <kbd>←</kbd> to go back, and <kbd>O</kbd> for the outline.

:::notes
Open the presenter, step through, then show the outline.
:::

### Jump and keep time
{id=s22}

> **Try it.** <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> go to the first and last slide, <kbd>S</kbd> skips the next one, <kbd>B</kbd> comes back from a jump, <kbd>1–9</kbd> open a grid card.

> **Try it.** <kbd>P</kbd> starts or pauses the timer, <kbd>T</kbd> sets the length; **Reset timer** is in the presenter's list (<kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>).

> **Try it.** <kbd>⇧R</kbd> records and <kbd>⇧P</kbd> pauses. <kbd>L</kbd> saves the run; at the last slide <kbd>↵</kbd> keeps it as a delivery.

:::notes
Five minutes go quickly. Start the timer before the first word.
:::

### Show and point
{id=s23}

> **Try it.** <kbd>R</kbd> reveals step by step, <kbd>F</kbd> focuses, <kbd>H</kbd> highlights, <kbd>I</kbd> points. <kbd>D</kbd> is the pen: <kbd>W</kbd> changes tool, <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> undoes a stroke, <kbd>X</kbd> and <kbd>⇧X</kbd> clear.

> **Try it.** <kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>−</kbd> size the text, <kbd>[</kbd> the previews. <kbd>Z</kbd> enlarges a picture or page, <kbd>V</kbd> a video; <kbd>M</kbd> plays media, <kbd>E</kbd> lets you use a page.

> **Try it.** In the presenter's list (<kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>): **Pen tool: Freehand** and **Clear highlights**.

:::notes
Point at one thing only. Clear the drawing before you move on.
:::

### Notes and help
{id=s24}

> **Try it.** <kbd>J</kbd> scrolls these notes. In the list (<kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>), **Notes: bottom** moves them; **Notes scroll automatically**, **Notes scroll faster** and **Notes scroll slower** run them.

> **Try it.** <kbd>?</kbd> shows the keys, <kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd> lists every command, <kbd>⌘⇧,</kbd> gives the highlighted one a new key, <kbd>C</kbd>, or the Guide button at the left of the bottom bar, shows this chapter beside the notes.

:::notes
These notes are only for you. The audience never sees them. Read the first line, look up, and say the rest in your own words.
:::

## 07 Share
{id=07-share}

> **Try it.** In All commands (<kbd>⌘⇧P</kbd>): **Share: handout** and **Share: HTML presentation** make files and show them in Finder. **Share: publish to Cloudflare Pages…** puts the handout online; **Share: for comments…** opens a sheet to share the talk for comments.

:::notes
Thank the room and say where the handout is.
:::
