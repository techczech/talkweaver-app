# TalkWeaver publishing and hosting

TalkWeaver is a content creation platform that allows publishing and sharing online on the user's self-hosted cloud service. Users must abide by the policies of their cloud providers when sharing.

## Possible future TalkWeaver hosting

In the future TalkWeaver may provide hosting services, and those will be for educational content only, excluding nudity or violence other than for the purposes of education or commentary with a clear educational purpose. Any content deemed non-compliant can be removed without notice or justification.

## Reporting an online content concern

Reports open the developer's public GitHub issue page. Do not include private talks, personal information, passwords, access tokens or logs. Nothing is attached automatically. For sensitive concerns or requests to remove content, contact the person or organisation hosting the content.

[Report an online content concern](https://github.com/techczech/talkweaver-app/issues)
